# Furiten 0.1.1

- Review: the day's due cards are one batch, rolling over at an hour you set.
- Updates come from Anomaly's release service instead of Sparkle, and Furiten is now signed by Anomaly. Furiten 0.1.0 can't update to this version by itself: download it once from https://anoma.ly/release/furiten/desktop/stable/macos-arm64-dmg, and later versions install themselves.
- Furiten Cloud is gone: devices sync with your desktop or your own `furiten-hub`.
- The macOS icon keeps its transparent margin.
